Questions
Good to know.

  

    
Can I use the icons in commercial projects?
Yes, in your own and in client projects, as long as you credit “Icons by Swoozh” somewhere visible. You may not sell, share or republish the icons as a set. The full terms are in the license.

    
How do I change the stroke width?
Outline icons have a stroke version from 0.75 to 2 px. In React, Vue and Svelte import from @swoozh/icons-react/stroke and set strokeWidth. The web component takes a stroke-width attribute. Filled icons and the icon font have a fixed weight.

    
Which formats are available?
SVG files, an SVG sprite, React, Vue, Svelte, a web component, Elm and an icon font. Packages are tree-shakeable, so you only ship the icons you import.

    
How do I find an icon?
Search by name, synonym or category. “like” finds the heart, “refund” finds return.

    
Are the icons accessible?
Icons are decorative by default (aria-hidden). Pass a title (or label on the web component) to expose a name to assistive technology.
